# Research evidence register

Sources: the supplied 11 September 2026 patent/research package, October Portfolio v1.4, QNTA Contract and QNTA Systems. Curated research descriptions omit patent-file identifiers, personal data and protected mechanisms. This register is not a finding of peer review, patent grant, inventorship or commercial release.

## R01 FQNM
The supplied continuum-dynamics paper studies quantised conservative transfer for supported conservation-law workloads. Interpret convergence and continuum reconstruction within the paper's assumptions and baseline. Its existence does not establish universal model training, every PDE, arbitrary backend speed or energy savings. FQNM is a research asset rather than a mandatory product dependency.

## R02 CRE
The CRE-SPD paper concerns structured Cholesky/inverse reconstruction and faithful real representation for admissible operators. It does not remove cubic worst-case dense complexity. State which structure, dimensions, precision, numerical error and implementation are compared. CRE may enable a suitable representation without being a mandatory component of AXIOM-TENSOR or QNTA.

## R03 AXIOM
The signed-XOR table-free multiplication paper distinguishes basis support updates from algebra-specific signs. Table-free basis operations do not remove the dependence of a sparse bilinear product on its stored terms. The paper names Park Junhu and Myungjoo Kang; coauthorship is not proof of patent inventorship or ownership.

## R04 Judge-CIA and related monitoring
The anonymous Judge-CIA paper is research evidence for its stated judge/monitoring setting. An anonymous manuscript does not identify an inventor. The package's mapping includes a related hallucination-monitoring specification, but that does not create an extra validated commercial product. One mapped application is represented by an R 1 DOCX because a filed specification PDF is missing; never label that substitute a verified filed PDF.

## R05 AXIOM-TENSOR
The supplied latest paper is dated 6 August 2026. Structured tensor/operator representation must be evaluated for parameter budget, approximation, memory, execution and task quality separately. Smaller representations are not themselves measured whole-model speedups or energy savings. AXIOM Compute is the validation-stage software offering; AXIOM Core is planned dedicated hardware/IP, not an already delivered runtime executing every research route.

## R06 SPADES
SPADES concerns physics-guided probabilistic correction of discretized PDE solutions. The supplied manuscript is not peer-reviewed evidence. It remains a research program outside the current product catalogue until a defined offering is established. Do not infer a general-purpose solver or universal correction benefit.

## R07 PRISM
PRISM separates acquisition, detection and delivery of decision-relevant observation from expensive reasoning. The newer finite panel reports 58.8–69.2% fewer observation-induced tokens,23.1–30.1% fewer model calls and 88.1–90.0% fewer delivered bytes. Interpret model sizes, episode durations, task success and observation fidelity together; token savings are not automatically financial or energy savings. Selected LLM-free workloads demonstrate observation behavior without proving model-call savings where no model was used. The older PRISM range belongs to an older experiment and is not a contradictory current guarantee.

## QNTA Contract and Systems
These describe the semantic contract and execution/state-management aspects of one QNTA architecture. QNTA Runtime is the feasibility-demonstrated software product. Toy-task learning, transfer tests on pretrained states and accelerator feasibility have different claim scopes. GPT-2 state-transfer evidence is not end-to-end GPT-2 training. Systems evidence includes hardware-dependent wins and negative NPU performance; do not generalize to every model/device or measured power benefit. Detailed contract/state representations remain protected and are not reproduced here.

Verify legal status, authorship, inventorship, ownership and disclosure independently. The earlier three-application AXIOM/CRE/FQNM statement is a dated subset.
