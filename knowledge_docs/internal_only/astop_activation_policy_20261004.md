# ASTOP software protection controls

Sources: retained Software Protection Policy v1.5 controls, updated by the explicit activation and continuity rules in Customer Journey v1.2, reconciled with LO v2.6 and Branch Agreement v1.4. Customer Journey v1.6 introduces trial and annual membership; revised complete legal/protection texts require approval before launch. The referenced full Software Protection v1.6.1 was not supplied; this is not a summary of that unseen document. This curated internal policy contains no identity-document images, private application identifiers, keys or protected implementation mechanisms.

Verify counterparty and email and accept trial terms before protected trial delivery. Payment is not a trial prerequisite. Annual membership requires explicit Join after the full seven-day trial, LO and recurring authorization, then verified payment. Treat profile claims as unverified until an approved process records evidence. For organizations verify registration, representative authority and administrator identity. Normalize email for matching; use opaque customer, order, license, build and activation identifiers in binaries and tokens. Never embed raw email or legal identity in the distributed executable.

Use the same approved signed production build for customers of a supported platform; personalize authenticated expiring delivery and licensing records, not the executable. Distribute only approved signed production builds through authenticated, short-lived delivery tied to a valid entitlement. Do not distribute raw debug binaries, source or reusable uncontrolled links. Preserve build hash, signing identity, platform, download event, license and activation traceability. Authorization must be checked when a link is redeemed as well as when it is issued.

Preserve the exact accepted LO/version/hash and purchaser snapshot, timestamp/session evidence and linked payment/license history. Do not mutate accepted agreements when current terms change. Trusted provider capture establishes paid status; screenshots and browser redirects do not. Delivery or license status must not grant unrelated Knowledge Core disclosure access.

Activation identifies the licensed seat and approved environment using opaque identifiers, never workload content. Three registered environments per named seat are allowed; a fourth requires explicit confirmed replacement. The ordinary renewal interval is seven days. Offline validity expires fourteen days from the last successful validation (not seven plus fourteen). Once expired, restore entitlement before a new session. Runtime validation must verify signatures, environment binding and expiry for new sessions while preserving completion of sessions/jobs validly started while entitled. Expiry or revocation alone must not terminate such a job mid-session.

Refund approval ends legal rights and blocks future delivery, activation and renewal before or with repayment; organization refunds normally cover all seats. Do not truncate or extend an already issued offline validity window on refund, confirmed chargeback, suspension or termination. Existing validly started jobs may finish. Revoked server records indicate no future issuance, not an instruction to kill a running job. Previously issued legacy tokens retain their existing expiry; renewal is required to obtain the updated contract. Record repayment pending durably and use a stable provider idempotency key. Refunds/chargebacks reverse reward accrual and paid invalid rewards require recovery accounting. Do not delay repayment until offline expiry.


Fraud handling requires auditable suspend, review, restore and revoke paths. Check self/related-account activity, circular referral patterns and invalid attribution before settlement. The new membership refund request window is fourteen days after the applicable payment. Branch settlement holds remain separate and require approved terms; neither elapsed time nor a trial start is payout authority. Branches refer customers and cannot collect funds, license software or independently distribute binaries.

Production launch remains gated on a tested payment/tax adapter, private verification operations, signed-build delivery, runtime enforcement, approved complete agreements and an end-to-end refund/revocation test. Merging application code does not establish these external services.


## Trial and annual entitlement controls
Trial start and expiry, named-user/environment scope and annual membership status must be included in signed entitlement data. Cap new activation authority at entitlement expiry; the ordinary fourteen-day offline ceiling must never extend a seven-day trial. Keep private signing keys only in the controlled itriX signing service; the runtime verifies signatures using public keys. Authentic code signatures prove origin, not license rights. Cancellation, refund and revocation must stop future authority as approved without changing other customers' access or fabricating instant offline erasure. Signing/licensing does not make copying, reverse engineering or IP extraction impossible. Pilot tests must cover invalid signatures, tampered builds, copied credentials, environment limits, trial expiry, recurring consent, renewal, cancellation, refund and revocation before broad launch.
