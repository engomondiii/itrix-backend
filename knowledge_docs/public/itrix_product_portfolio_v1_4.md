# itriX product portfolio and technology architecture v1.4
Source: Product Portfolio and Technology Architecture v1.4, 2 October 2026. Public summary excludes application identifiers and protected mechanisms.

## Products and development stages
ASTOP is observation software based on PRISM; implementation is demonstrated.
AXIOM Compute is computational software based on AXIOM and AXIOM-TENSOR; it is in validation. It represents suitable tensor/operator workloads in structured form. Quality, memory and speed require workload-specific validation.
AXIOM Core is planned dedicated hardware or hardware IP implementing validated AXIOM structures. Delivery depends on hardware development and validation.
QNTA Runtime is runtime software implementing QNTA Inference-Based Training Architecture through inference operations and explicit state control. Feasibility is demonstrated on tested infrastructure; support and delivery are agreed separately.

## Names and relationships
The product families ASTOP, AXIOM and QNTA group offerings and are not additional products. ALPHA Compute and ALPHA Core are historical names; the current full names are AXIOM Compute and AXIOM Core.
PRISM, AXIOM and AXIOM-TENSOR are technologies. QNTA abbreviates QNTA Inference-Based Training Architecture. QNTA Contract and QNTA Systems are specification and execution/state-management aspects of that single technology, not separate products. QNTA Core is only a future hardware designation.
CRE is an enabling technology; FQNM is a research asset; SPADES is a research program outside the product portfolio until a defined offering is validated. They are not mandatory dependencies of AXIOM-TENSOR or QNTA.
Observe, represent, learn and execute describe capabilities, not a required sales or processing sequence. ASTOP proof is not a universal prerequisite for discussing a separately qualified AXIOM or QNTA workload.

## Evidence boundaries
Stage basis is the technical reply of 14 September 2026, retained in the October portfolio. QNTA filing was in progress at that verification boundary; no confirmed application number is supplied. Do not infer a grant, release date or unrestricted technical disclosure. AXIOM-TENSOR parameter reduction is not proof of end-to-end speed or quality retention. QNTA feasibility is not universal acceleration or measured energy savings.
