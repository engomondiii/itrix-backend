# ASTOP customer journey — Store distribution, October 2026
Source basis: 8 October meeting decisions and the subsequent Temporary ASTOP Store Routing Rule supplied by the project owner. Current public commercial summary, not a Store listing or legal agreement.

## Discover → Understand → Ask → Evaluate → Qualify
The website and AI-Powered Sales Engine explain ASTOP, answer questions, assess workload relevance and agree the appropriate next step. ASTOP observes decision-relevant state changes to reduce unnecessary repeated observation and reasoning; results depend on the workload, integration and required events. Open product questions do not require a site account, legal identity documents or payment.

## Retail before Store launch
ASTOP is being prepared for release through the Apple App Store and Microsoft Store. Until itriX confirms a particular listing is live and ready for commercial use, do not provide purchase links, activate Store buttons, offer website checkout or distribute installers. Invite an interested visitor to register interest or request an availability notification. Interest registration is not a purchase, reservation, trial entitlement or confirmed release date.

## Retail after confirmed launch
Only direct visitors to the confirmed live listing for their relevant platform. One live Store does not establish availability in the other. Retail purchase, installation, updates and Store distribution take place through Apple or Microsoft, not the itriX website. Exact supported systems, release availability and listing terms must be checked; do not invent Linux availability or a Store URL.

## Strategic Enterprise Validation
A strategic or complex enterprise opportunity can proceed to separately scoped Strategic Enterprise Validation. Clarify the representative workload, decision, baseline, expected evidence, security/deployment constraints and responsible contact. Offer the PoC application when the visitor chooses it; submission is a request for review, not approval, automatic NDA disclosure or a promise of free engineering. Retail waitlisting must not block enterprise discussions.

## Evidence and next steps
Explain first, then suggest one relevant next step. Preserve no-fit and stop outcomes. Compare quality, necessary events, latency, tokens, calls and observation overhead against a meaningful baseline. Do not present internal percentages as guaranteed customer savings or finite tests as 24-hour trials. Historical accepted customer agreements remain historical rights, not current acquisition offers.
