# Current platform and commercial governance

Sources: October Portfolio v1.4, ASTOP Customer Journey v 1.0, LO v2.6, Software Protection v1.5 and Branch Agreement v1.4. Retains applicable controls from the earlier MVP acceptance feedback while superseding its historical product names. Implementation plans, theme guides, wireframes and old source requests are not current client-answer evidence.

Maintain public-safe discovery without forcing a product, collecting identity documents in chat or creating a later commercial stage from technical sophistication. ASTOP, AXIOM Compute and QNTA Runtime have distinct relevance and readiness; AXIOM Core remains planned hardware/IP. Observe/represent/learn/execute is not a mandatory sales sequence. A separately qualified AXIOM workload does not require prior ASTOP value. A generic keyword or questionnaire hypothesis never establishes commercial qualification.

Retail ASTOP has verified individual/organization LO routes. Protected enterprise evaluation remains separately scoped. Confidentiality agreement, consent to an evaluation, permission for a PoC, content disclosure authorization and production entitlement are distinct. NDA signatures never automatically unlock restricted documents. Avoid forcing a PoC label when a controlled evaluation is the selected stage. Keep customer-facing outputs free of internal scores, fee-delegation logic and confidential strategy.

Define workload, baseline, task quality, scope, relevant hardware and success/failure criteria before evaluation. An evaluation may end with no fit or no further action. Confirm monetary benefits only from measured scope, and distinguish published research from a client's own proof. Record negative results and costs, not just favorable percentages.

Specific ASTOP LO pricing overrides illustrative journey prices and old generic value-participation examples. Non-ASTOP enterprise fees and rights remain separately negotiated and explicitly agreed; no research capability grants a license, exclusive rights, perpetual term, ownership or support bundle. Existing assessment fee review/delegation cannot waive substantive identity, scope and authorization gates.

For workload transfer, establish permitted use, authorized recipients, security/access controls, data handling and evaluation scope as appropriate. Protect internal mechanisms and personal information. Preserve exact executed agreement evidence; do not silently edit accepted platform terms to rename products. Explain historical names with separate current terminology guidance.

Keep checkout and Branch execution disabled where approved agreements, service integrations or required evidence are absent. The sales deck proposes options, not approved freemium, trial, hosted-service, public installer or package-manager policies. Unresolved legal/payout fields require completion and authorized review, not invented defaults presented as contract terms.
