# Historical ASTOP entitlement support boundary
Updated November 5, 2026. Round 7 does not approve new trial, payment or installer issuance. Do not apply historical three-environment, seven-day validation or fourteen-day offline policies to an unconfirmed release. Packaging, signing, licensing and supported environments require itriX release approval.

Keep historical accepted agreements, issued entitlement expiries, audit trails, cancellation and refund obligations unchanged. Historical support must verify the specific customer's accepted terms rather than converting them to a new plan. Refund approval and repayment are distinct operations with idempotent processing; do not invent payment completion or destroy evidence. No token, role, payment or NDA grants unrelated Knowledge Core access. Signing secrets and private identity data never become public or customer-download content.
