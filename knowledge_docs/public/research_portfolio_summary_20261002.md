# Research portfolio and publication boundaries
Sources: supplied September patent/research mapping and papers; QNTA Contract and QNTA Systems; October portfolio v1.4. This public summary excludes filing identifiers and protected implementation detail. Papers are research evidence; peer review, patent grant and commercial readiness are not inferred.

AXIOM signed-XOR work separates basis support updates from algebra-specific signs. Table-free basis multiplication does not eliminate the usual bilinear dependence of sparse multiplication on stored terms. The supplied paper names Park Junhu and Myungjoo Kang; coauthorship is not patent inventorship.
CRE concerns faithful real embedding and structured SPD reduction/inverse reconstruction on admissible operators. It retains cubic worst-case dense complexity; do not claim a subcubic general inverse.
FQNM concerns quantised conservative transfer rules for suitable physical/dynamical systems. Convergence conditions and prototype baselines matter; it is not a general model-training product.
AXIOM-TENSOR is structured representation with workload-dependent compression, approximation quality and execution costs. Parameter savings do not establish end-to-end acceleration.
SPADES is a non-peer-reviewed research program for physics-guided probabilistic correction of discretized PDE solutions. It is not a current product. Judge-CIA and hallucination-monitoring materials remain research, not additions to the product catalogue.

QNTA Inference-Based Training Architecture separates prescribed-resolution training semantics from the physical implementation. QNTA Contract and QNTA Systems describe the same technology. The supplied evidence distinguishes complete toy-task learning, transfer tests on pretrained model states and hardware feasibility. Tests involving GPT-2 states are not end-to-end GPT-2 training. Systems results include hardware-dependent winners and negative NPU performance evidence. Inference infrastructure feasibility is not autonomous training on every inference-only device or a universal speed/energy claim. Detailed QNTA mechanisms remain subject to explicit disclosure authorization.
