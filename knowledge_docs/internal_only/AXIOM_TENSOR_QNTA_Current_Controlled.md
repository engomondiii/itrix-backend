# AXIOM-TENSOR and QNTA — current controlled assessment routes

Source basis: ITRIX Core Technology Brief, 28 August 2026. This synthesis intentionally excludes unrelated SPADES material and preserves the September 2026 product taxonomy: AXIOM-TENSOR and QNTA are technologies under ALPHA Compute, not separately purchasable products.

## AXIOM-TENSOR
AXIOM-TENSOR explores representing selected AI model/operator structure in a smaller form. Current internal evidence includes examples across multiple model categories where smaller representations retained core functions or output characteristics, and a productization demo is in progress to evaluate quality, memory reduction and executability together at whole-model level. The likely route is selective replacement rather than uniformly shrinking every part of a model.

Claim boundary: exact representation, reduced-budget representation, operator/layer results, full-model results, representation size, execution memory, runtime and energy are distinct claims. Parameter or representation reduction must not be translated directly into speed, cost or energy advantage. Applicability is an assessment hypothesis until the actual workload passes its evidence gate.

## QNTA
QNTA explores learning/adaptation on execution environments and devices that are often oriented toward inference. The supplied controlled brief reports validated execution paths across several hardware families and core computation on an NPU, while explicitly stating that this does not establish immediate high speed or power efficiency on every device.

Claim boundary: do not claim universal NPU training, any-model/any-device training, universal edge training, or GPU elimination. A customer-facing next step is a task/runtime/device eligibility assessment under ALPHA Compute.

## Combination boundary
AXIOM-TENSOR and QNTA have separate internal validation paths. Their combination is a future strategic direction, not a presently integrated and validated product capability.
