# ASTOP membership and acceptance summary
Source: Customer Journey v1.6. Current membership policy summary, not the revised License Order itself. The approved exact agreement displayed and accepted by the customer governs legal rights. Do not claim approved checkout exists merely because this summary is available.

## Annual pricing and free trial
Seven-day free trial with no payment. Join only after the full trial and explicit customer election, acceptance and automatic-renewal authorization. Individual: USD 20 per year for one named user; Organization: USD 16 per user per year, minimum two users. Eligible Branch referral: USD 18 per year for an individual. Five organization seats cost USD 80 per year, including with a qualifying referral. Apply the greater eligible discount without stacking; referral attribution may remain without a further discount. No perpetual software ownership is implied.

## Identity and evidence
Verified email and purchaser identity precede trial entitlement. Organization access requires verified entity, representative authority, administrator and named users. Use approved private verification, not chat uploads. Preserve the exact trial terms and License Order, hashes, version, identity snapshot, acceptance timestamps and recurring authorization. Do not rewrite already accepted orders, including their earlier refund conditions. A registered account or browser payment redirect is not confirmed payment.

## Annual renewal, cancellation and refunds
Annual membership starts at successful first payment and renews each year only under authorized terms. Show status, paid-through date and renewal/cancellation options; provide appropriate renewal notice. Requests for refunds are permitted within fourteen calendar days after the applicable membership payment, subject to the LO and mandatory law. Cancellation of future renewal is separate from refund approval. Approval and repayment are separate auditable steps; prevent duplicate repayment. Do not fabricate detailed cancellation or refund mechanics absent approved terms. Customer proof and reported outcomes are not automatic payment, cancellation or refund instructions.

## Installation and protection
Authenticated controlled delivery follows valid trial entitlement without payment, or valid paid membership. Trial activation tokens must not authorize new sessions after trial expiry. Protection Policy governs environment counts, periodic validation, offline/session continuity and revocation; calendar-year billing is not validation cadence. Retained standard technical controls are three environments per named seat, confirmed replacement before a fourth, seven-day validation renewal and at most fourteen-day offline validity, always bounded by trial or annual entitlement expiry and approved current policy. Signing keys never go to customers. Licensing identifiers do not include workload content; this is not a promise about all data flows in third-party integrations.
