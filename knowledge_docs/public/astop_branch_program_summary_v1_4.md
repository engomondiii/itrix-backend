# ASTOP Branch program

Source: itriX ASTOP Branch Agreement v1.4, 2 October 2026. Curated policy summary. The supplied agreement has unresolved execution fields; launch and acceptance require a completed approved agreement. The confidential strategy document and undecided sales options are not independent entitlement sources.

A Branch is a qualified referral partner, not an agent authorized to bind itriX, collect payments, grant licenses or distribute protected builds. An existing ASTOP licensee applies, itriX reviews and approves, then the applicant accepts a separate exact Branch agreement. Only after those events may an opaque Branch identifier and referral code become active. Signing up, invitations, recruitment or paying an enrollment fee do not earn rewards.

A parent Branch is established only from the applicant's earlier qualifying paid purchase attributed to that parent's eligible code, followed by later approval and agreement acceptance. Do not create parent links from invitations alone. Use one eligible lineage per transaction, with a maximum of five compensated levels. Exclude self-referrals, circular or reciprocal arrangements and controlled-account abuse. Verified counterparties and transaction/fraud evidence matter; a different email is not proof of an independent customer.

The five rates are 10%,5%,2.5%,1.25%,0.75% of eligible actually collected net license fees, excluding taxes, refunds and chargebacks. They sum to 19.5%. An eligible USD 18 base accrues USD 3.51 across all five levels before payout rounding. Do not apply inconsistent strategy illustrations as a contractual schedule. Freeze eligible attribution for each order; later profile or hierarchy changes must not rewrite historical allocation.

Rewards remain subject to the Branch Agreement settlement hold and settlement/fraud review. The historical 30-day Branch hold is not the current customer refund window: new annual membership permits refund requests within fourteen days after the applicable payment. The updated Branch operating package must be approved before new membership rewards can be released. Trial starts and enrollment do not earn rewards. Elapsed time alone is insufficient authorization to pay. Reverse unpaid invalid rewards and record clawback obligations where invalid rewards were already paid. Release, payout cadence, minimum amount, currency and provider depend on completed approved terms and real settlement records; no automatic bank-transfer service is promised by the ledger.

Eligible customer discount is 10%, without stacking with the 20% organization seat discount. A larger organization discount does not by itself remove otherwise qualifying Branch attribution. Claims, publications and use of branding must stay within approved scope and supported evidence.
