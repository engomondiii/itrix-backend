# ASTOP License Order summary

Source: ASTOP Website License Order Agreement, internal version 2.6. This curated summary explains the supplied policy. It is not the executed LO; the exact reviewed order displayed and accepted by the purchaser governs.

## Price and entitlement
An individual order is one named seat at USD 20. Organization orders begin at two named seats at USD 16 per seat. An eligible Branch referral gives a 10% customer discount. Apply the greater eligible discount, never add or multiply discounts; organization purchases may retain qualifying Branch attribution even when the organization discount determines the price. Taxes, payable total and other order-specific particulars must be stated and verified in the actual checkout. These prices do not establish a subscription period, perpetual rights or unlimited usage not expressed in the LO.

A verified individual counterparty requires legal name, country, address and identity verification. A verified organization requires registered legal identity, jurisdiction, registration number/address, representative and authority. Verified email is also required. These details are collected through approved verification processes, not public chat. The representative must have authority to bind the organization and an administrator manages identified seat users.

Payment grants only the agreed software license rights after all prerequisites, not ownership of the software or intellectual property. Seats cannot be pooled or shared; reassignment terminates the previous assignment's rights. There is no automatic sublicense, resale or right to operate a third-party hosted service. Updates and support are included only when the order expressly includes them. Binary downloads and activations remain tied to active entitlements and approved environments.

## Acceptance evidence
Retain the purchaser snapshot, exact LO text/version/hash, order amount, acceptance time and session evidence, and links to confirmed payment and license records. Changes to website terms or a purchaser profile do not rewrite previously accepted evidence. A draft contract with incomplete fields must not be represented as executed or ready for acceptance.

## Refunds and rights
The standard request window is 30 calendar days. A request initiates review; it is not proof of approved repayment. Approval revokes the affected rights before or with repayment, including use, download and activation renewal. Organization refunds normally cover the whole order; partial treatment requires explicit approval and supporting implementation. Prevent double repayment with provider idempotency. Reverse or recover invalid referral rewards when fees are refunded or charged back.

Customer Journey v1.2 clarifies renewal every seven days and offline validity up to fourteen days from the last successful validation. Approval stops renewal without truncating or restarting that existing window. Jobs started while entitled may finish; expiry prevents new sessions until entitlement is restored. Three environments per named seat require confirmed replacement before a fourth. These clarifications do not rewrite the exact previously accepted LO. Mandatory statutory consumer rights remain applicable. The supplied LO includes Korea/KCAB dispute provisions with mandatory-law carve-outs; do not substitute this summary for reviewed jurisdiction-specific terms or advice.
