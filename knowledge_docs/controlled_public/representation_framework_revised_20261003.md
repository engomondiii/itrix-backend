# Representation framework — revised older synthesis

Source: Unified Mathematical View v2.0 and older AXIOM/CRE/FQNM overviews, reconciled with October Portfolio v1.4. This edited derivative retains the common conceptual framework without a compulsory product pipeline or a universal theorem claim.

Representation, observation, transfer, execution and reconstruction are questions to ask about a computation. They are not mandatory purchase stages. The state may be richer than the observed output; the execution form may differ from the original mathematical expression; reconstruction must preserve the meaning required by the task under stated conditions.

AXIOM asks about algebraic state and operations, including what the observation leaves unrepresented. CRE asks whether an eligible operator can be represented in a suitable real structured form while preserving relevant invariants. FQNM asks whether selected conservative dynamics should be executed as transfer before continuum reconstruction. These perspectives are related but are neither identical nor automatic prerequisites for each other.

PRISM concerns decision-relevant observation before expensive reasoning. AXIOM-TENSOR concerns structured tensor/operator representation. QNTA Contract and Systems describe aspects of one QNTA architecture; they do not become two separately sold products. SPADES is a distinct research program. Shared language about state or observation does not prove shared algorithms, compatible implementations or one combined speedup.

For a proposed combination, document what each component contributes, transformation and storage costs, interfaces, recovery requirements, task-quality criteria and an ablation against the baseline. Measure combined effects rather than adding or multiplying separate benchmark percentages. Preserve negative results, crossover conditions and required events. A conceptual synthesis does not establish deployability or legal rights.

Current products are ASTOP, AXIOM Compute, planned AXIOM Core and QNTA Runtime at their stated maturity stages. CRE is enabling technology; FQNM and SPADES are research assets. Detailed mathematical mechanisms and partner mappings require explicit disclosure authorization. Newer papers govern their specific experimental claims; older explanations remain supporting context rather than an authority to override current products or contracts.
