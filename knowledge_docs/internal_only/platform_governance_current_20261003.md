## Current routing and execution safeguards
Ordinary ASTOP acquisition and separately scoped enterprise validation are concurrent routes. Discovery checks plausibility; customer proof follows activation. Company size alone does not justify enterprise handling. Record the concrete reason ordinary access is insufficient; before protected work require a defined workload, technical and decision owners, comparable baseline, fidelity and success/no-go criteria, security/data authorization, an approved effort allowance, next decision and deadline. Return standard cases to ordinary access and stop cases without sufficient value or justified effort. Existing NDA, agreement and disclosure boundaries still apply.

Keep each active opportunity assigned to an owner and next decision, with a deadline and continue/stop rationale. Bound engineering commitments by approved capacity, workload, sponsorship and proof criteria. AI assistance may explain approved offerings and gather approved feedback, but cannot independently grant protected rights, bespoke commitments or unapproved waivers. Record friction, unanswered questions, proof outcomes, support and refund causes for human review; customer feedback must not automatically become authoritative shared knowledge.

Company rollout priorities are not customer product prerequisites. Mathematical or PDE keywords do not establish a commercial product fit; route unvalidated research assets to research review. AXIOM Core remains planned hardware/IP following validated software evidence; QNTA Runtime remains a supported-runtime feasibility offering. Any ambiguous table does not override consistent portfolio definitions. Negotiated non-ASTOP economics remain deal-specific and outside public default pricing.

Customer Journey v1.6 delegates detailed protection mechanics to the Protection Policy and legal terms to the applicable agreement. Record trial proof and membership decisions with separate customer-reported measurements and qualitative feedback. Review reusable learning before shared-knowledge publication. Journey references to approved advocates do not independently authorize bypassing the current Branch Agreement.
