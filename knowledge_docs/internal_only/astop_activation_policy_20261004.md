# Historical ASTOP entitlement support boundary
Updated 19 October 2026. The Store-led model supersedes new website trial, payment and installer issuance. Do not apply old three-environment, seven-day validation or fourteen-day offline policies to an unapproved Store release. Store packaging/runtime/licensing is the responsibility of itriX and its selected packaging specialist.

Keep historical accepted agreements, issued entitlement expiries, audit trails, cancellation and refund obligations unchanged.
