# ASTOP software protection controls

Source: ASTOP Software Protection Policy v1.5, reconciled with LO v2.6 and Branch Agreement v1.4. This curated internal policy contains no identity-document images, private application identifiers, keys or protected implementation mechanisms.

Verify the legal counterparty and email before LO acceptance, payment, licensing, delivery and activation. Treat profile claims as unverified until an approved process records evidence. For organizations verify registration, representative authority and administrator identity. Normalize email for matching; use opaque customer, order, license, build and activation identifiers in binaries and tokens. Never embed raw email or legal identity in the distributed executable.

Distribute only approved signed production builds through authenticated, short-lived delivery tied to a valid entitlement. Do not distribute raw debug binaries, source or reusable uncontrolled links. Preserve build hash, signing identity, platform, download event, license and activation traceability. Authorization must be checked when a link is redeemed as well as when it is issued.

Preserve the exact accepted LO/version/hash and purchaser snapshot, timestamp/session evidence and linked payment/license history. Do not mutate accepted agreements when current terms change. Trusted provider capture establishes paid status; screenshots and browser redirects do not. Delivery or license status must not grant unrelated Knowledge Core disclosure access.

Activation identifies the licensed seat and approved environment using opaque identifiers. Enforce reasonable configured environment limits, signed tokens, expiry, periodic validation and bounded offline grace. Seven-day validation is a suggested policy setting, not a claim of universal technical enforcement. The production runtime must actually verify signatures, environment binding, expiry and revocation at renewal. Document supported platforms and unavailable telemetry accurately.

Refund approval revokes rights before or with repayment; organization refunds normally revoke all seats. Block new downloads, updates/support rights where applicable, activation and renewal. Record pending repayment durably and retry with a stable idempotency key. Refunds and chargebacks reverse related reward accrual; previously paid invalid rewards require recovery accounting. Disconnected machines enforce expiry or the next validation, not instantaneous remote erasure.

Fraud handling requires auditable suspend, review, restore and revoke paths. Check self/related-account activity, circular referral patterns and invalid attribution before settlement. The 30-day refund period is a minimum settlement gate, not automatic payout authority. Branches refer customers and cannot collect funds, license software or independently distribute binaries.

Production launch remains gated on a tested payment/tax adapter, private verification operations, signed-build delivery, runtime enforcement, approved complete agreements and an end-to-end refund/revocation test. Merging application code does not establish these external services.
