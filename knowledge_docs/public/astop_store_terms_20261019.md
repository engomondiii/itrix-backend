# ASTOP Store pricing and purchase boundary
Source: agreed 8 October 2026 meeting, qualified by the supplied temporary Store routing rule.

Working retail membership pricing: USD 10 per month or USD 100 per year. Twelve monthly payments total USD 120; the annual working price is USD 20 lower, approximately two months of monthly membership. This is the agreed working standard, not evidence of a live Store listing, final local taxes or platform currency conversion.

The itriX website does not sell or distribute ASTOP directly. Apple App Store and Microsoft Store handle retail transactions and distribution after itriX confirms each relevant listing is live. Until then, register interest or request an availability notification; strategic opportunities may request Strategic Enterprise Validation.

Do not offer the superseded individual/organization seat prices, Branch discounts, website trial-first enrollment, site payment or direct installer download as current retail terms. Do not infer Store trial length, refunds, renewal/cancellation rules, family sharing, organization discounts, environment limits or licensing conditions from earlier website agreements. These need the applicable approved Store listing and terms. No current Store listing was supplied with this update.

Existing customers' accepted terms and payment history must not be rewritten. Account support for historical obligations is separate from new retail acquisition. The AI explains and qualifies; it cannot issue an entitlement, take payment, guarantee approval or promise access based on a chat message.
