# ASTOP customer journey — v1.5

Source: ASTOP Customer Journey v1.5 (Condensed), supplied 5 October 2026; reconciled with License Order v2.6. Curated derivative, not an executed agreement or evidence that checkout and production activation services are live.

## Discover → Acquire → Activate → Prove → Decide → Continue

### Discover: plausibility, not qualification
Check four things: repeated observation of a long-running task; sparse meaningful changes; accessible evidence through logs, APIs, files, processes or counters; and a feasible comparable baseline after purchase. A one-shot task, cheap checks or sufficient existing webhooks may offer little incremental benefit. No fit and leaving without buying are valid outcomes. This is a plausibility check; genuine qualification happens through real workload proof after acquisition and activation.

ASTOP continues low-level sensing while reducing unnecessary reasoning wakeups. It does not replace the runtime or scheduler. Missing telemetry is unavailable, not measured zero. The finite-panel research results do not promise customer savings, universal task coverage or a 24-hour endurance test.

### Acquire: two ordinary license types
Individual means one verified person and one named authorized user. Organization means a verified legal entity, authorized representative, designated license administrator and two or more named seats. USD 20 for an individual; USD 16 per organization seat. An eligible Branch referral gives 10% off the individual price (USD 18). The greater eligible discount applies without ordinary stacking; organization pricing can retain Branch attribution.

Verify the counterparty and email; display and accept the exact electronic License Order; satisfy the payment condition; issue License ID and entitlement; provide controlled download. Browser redirects, account registration and a referral code alone never establish entitlement. Collect identity through approved verification processes, not chat. Ordinary acquisition does not require a sales meeting or protected enterprise evaluation. If online checkout is unavailable, state that and contact itriX for the next step.

Strategic Enterprise Validation is exception handling, not an additional Customer Journey stage or a third ordinary license. Large organizations may use ordinary acquisition. A specific security, procurement, private deployment, high-volume, extended-offline or protected-scope need may justify separately approved review. The exception returns to the same licensing architecture; no automatic bespoke rights arise.

### Activate: controlled delivery → install → activate → connect → run
Provide authenticated controlled delivery, require a valid account and License ID, activate the environment and create local entitlement. ASTOP is licensed, not sold; possession of an installer does not confer a right to run it. The completion signal is installed, connected and running under valid entitlement.

The journey exposes customer actions and status. Signed production builds, renewal cadence, offline validity, Authorized Environment limits, revocation and licensing telemetry belong to the Protection Policy. Version 1.5 does not establish new numerical protection limits or cancel earlier compatible protection controls; do not invent changes from their omission in this condensed journey. Linux x86_64 and ARM64 remain distinct; Windows through WSL is not native Windows GUI support. Missing telemetry remains unavailable rather than measured zero.

### Prove: a comparable real workload
After purchase and activation, define decisions, observable predicates and required events that cannot be missed or delayed. Version the baseline; keep workload, model, host, software, hardware and acceptance criteria comparable. Check decision fidelity first: task success, missed required events, false wakeups and decision latency. Then measure observation-induced tokens, model calls, delivered bytes and observer CPU, memory, I/O and classification cost. Distinguish estimates and qualitative feedback from measurements. Preserve unknowns, negative results and baseline corrections; rerun affected comparisons. Never invent tests or treat published results as customer proof.

### Decide: record the explicit outcome
Present a comparable workload-specific result honestly and separate reliable measurements from qualitative observations. Record the customer's selected outcome: continue, tune, try another workload, expand, stop or refund where eligible. Continue requires preserved decisions and positive system-level value. Tune predicates or thresholds when potentially suitable; check materially different workloads before expansion. No-fit and stop are legitimate outcomes.

A recorded decision is customer feedback, not independently verified proof, a new entitlement, an executed purchase or an approved refund. A refund decision points to the applicable order refund path; it does not itself submit or approve the request. Timing, legal termination, renewal stop, offline validity, running-session treatment and future access remain governed by the License Order and Protection Policy. The journey does not restate those mechanics.

### Continue
Monitor health and value, tune as needed, and reprove a materially different workload before expansion. Record friction, support needs, negative proof and reasons to stop through approved feedback channels, without exposing raw workloads. Buying ASTOP is not a prerequisite for independently relevant AXIOM Compute, AXIOM Core or QNTA Runtime inquiries.

### Optional advocacy and Branch participation
After successful use, a satisfied licensee may share experience voluntarily without creating a referral-reward right; advocacy is optional and is not a prerequisite for Branch application. Journey v1.5 describes application by an eligible licensee or approved advocate. Eligibility and reward rights are governed by the separate Branch Agreement, and status begins only after itriX approval and execution. An approved advocate may ask itriX for an eligibility review; do not treat this journey as authority to bypass the current agreement’s qualifying-purchase controls. Approved Branches receive an attributable opaque code. No rewards arise merely from clicks, registration, recruiting Branches or creating a code. Existing agreement eligibility, lineage, settlement and refund safeguards apply. Neither route is necessary to use ASTOP.

## Document boundaries and continued learning
Customer Journey governs the customer path and stage transitions. Protection Policy governs entitlement mechanics, controlled delivery and activation. License Order governs legal use rights, scope, payment and customer obligations. Branch Agreement governs participation, attribution and rewards. A newer journey does not silently rewrite an accepted agreement or authorize a broader Branch programme.

During continued use, maintain entitlement health, applicable updates/support and visibility into deployment value; escalate to humans only when deployment, support or strategic needs justify it. Return reusable learning through review into shared knowledge. Raw workload data, customer-specific notes and unverified claims are not automatically public evidence. The optional growth loop is Continue → Advocate and/or Become a Branch → Refer → New Discover. Branches introduce customers to itriX; customers contract directly with and pay itriX, not a reseller. A referred customer becomes a sub-Branch only through separate enrollment.
