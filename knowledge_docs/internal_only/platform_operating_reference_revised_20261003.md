# Platform operating reference — retained baseline controls

Sources: older platform architecture, functional specification, playbook, build/UX guidelines, visitor personas, briefing template and operations milestones. This revised derivative retains operational principles that remain useful. October portfolio, journey, LO, protection and Branch sources govern changed products and commerce. Old schedules, unapproved financial targets, completed setup instructions and historical product definitions are not current requirements.

## Separate public, client and staff responsibilities
Public discovery helps a visitor describe a problem and understand bounded evidence. The client workspace holds that customer's agreed next steps, documents and engagement state. Staff tools support review, ownership, authorized disclosures and audit. A richer client answer must not expose internal scoring, partner strategy, waiver reasoning, private customer records or restricted technical mechanisms. Staff-dashboard implementation is owned separately from this backend/web change.

Authenticate workspace access through the existing session mechanism. Scope resources to their owner and authorized role. A product-route label, NDA signature, verified email, payment or knowledge tier is not a substitute for another control. Visitor attachments remain session-scoped context and must not enter the shared Knowledge Core or another customer's retrieval.

## Knowledge and response construction
Keep source authority, verification date, claim domain, disclosure tier and currentness separate. Use retrieval within permitted namespaces and audiences, ground claims in source evidence, and retain the qualification language that limits a result. Exclude retired claims even if old vectors remain. If approved evidence is absent, say what is unknown and ask a focused question rather than inventing capability, patent status, a price or an agreement.

Separate deterministic state/authorization rules from generated wording. Product hypotheses, lead signals and conversational sophistication do not advance a commercial stage. Preserve consent to each selected next step. A result page should reflect the visitor's problem, a bounded interpretation, relevant evidence and one appropriate next action; it must not treat a model guess as approved eligibility.

## Different visitors need different explanations
A technical buyer needs workload fit and integration questions; an evaluator needs methods and evidence limits; an executive needs scope and value assumptions; a hardware/cloud partner needs interface/IP questions; an investor needs dated corporate and research evidence; media needs clear public terminology; a student or general visitor needs education without pressure to buy. Do not force all of them through the same product or enterprise evaluation sequence.

Maintain Korean/English consistency of product identity, maturity, pricing and disclosure boundaries. Preserve historic identifiers and exact accepted agreement versions. Use the retained Atelier Indigo design guide for visual consistency; a terminology update is not a mandate to redesign unrelated screens.

## Technical evaluation and briefing
Before using customer material, establish permitted use, recipients, security/access controls and scope. Define workload, baseline, task quality, hardware, budget, success/failure criteria and evidence to retain. Version baseline changes and rerun affected comparisons rather than freezing an invalid baseline. Distinguish a scoped evaluation, a PoC, consent to transfer data, content authorization and production license rights.

A briefing records the customer's stated problem, facts vs hypotheses, chosen scope, evidence limits, unresolved questions, owner and agreed next action. It does not reveal hidden scores, invent an opening offer or convert an evaluation into a license. The retained acceptance feedback is historical test evidence and a source of regression cases; it is not a report of new testing or the authority for today's ASTOP retail price.

## Delivery, operations and acceptance
Keep clear release ownership, reviewable source changes, tests for authorization/state transitions, environment configuration, migration checks, rollback and audit. A repository commit is not a deployed integration. Demonstrate configured checkout, verified payment, signed delivery, activation, refund/revocation and attribution before enabling retail commerce. Preserve accepted legal/payment history through failures and retries.

Human review remains necessary where evidence, agreements or exceptional commercial decisions are incomplete. Escalation must identify the gap and owner without manufacturing approval. Existing enterprise assessment fee treatment cannot waive identity, workload or substantive authorization gates. Preserve the explicit no-fit/stop outcome and avoid automatic progression after an earlier successful stage.
