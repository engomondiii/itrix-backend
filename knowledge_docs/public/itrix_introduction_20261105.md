# itriX company introduction — November 2026
Source: client-supplied itriX_Introduction.pptx (six slides), reconciled with portfolio v1.7. Public company explanation; not confidential investor projections.
itriX develops computational AI infrastructure by improving the way computation is represented and executed before adding more hardware. Mathematical Intelligence is the company's technology philosophy: research mathematical structure, translate it into representations, implement and validate on relevant workloads. The founding team is rooted in Seoul National University mathematics and led by Myungjoo Kang. This background is not proof of universal performance or ownership of every invention.
ASTOP uses PRISM for observation before reasoning; implementation is demonstrated. AXIOM Compute targets suitable structured computation and is in validation. AXIOM Core is planned dedicated hardware or IP. QNTA Runtime implements inference-based learning with demonstrated feasibility. These maturity descriptions do not establish a release date, customer entitlement or supported hardware list. Observation, representation, learning and execution are distinct capability areas, not a mandatory product bundle.
