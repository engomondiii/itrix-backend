# iTrix Evaluation Methodology — NDA Only

## Proof
The evaluation methodology defines how fit is turned into evidence: representative workload
selection, the measurements taken, and how results are interpreted honestly. This is the
mechanism by which qualitative fit becomes something a client can act on.

## Solution
The methodology ties directly to the solution: an evaluation is scoped to the specific
pressure a team named, so the result speaks to their actual bottleneck rather than a
generic benchmark.

## Commercialization
This methodology applies to separately scoped enterprise evaluations. It is not a universal prerequisite for ASTOP retail purchase under LO v2.6. Later enterprise scope and rights require explicit agreement, and claims must remain within measured evidence.
