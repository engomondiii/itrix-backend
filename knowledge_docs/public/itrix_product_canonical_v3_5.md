## Canonical product catalogue

itriX currently has three products:

1. **ASTOP** — A System Trans-Observation Projector. The observation product. It operationalizes the PRISM observation domain for computational systems and agentic AI workflows.
2. **ALPHA Compute** — the independent software computational infrastructure product. It applies eligible computational technologies to suitable workloads and can produce software-layer value without ALPHA Core.
3. **ALPHA Core** — the separate optional hardware product. It is considered only when validated software-layer evidence justifies deeper hardware implementation or acceleration.

No fourth product is implied by the technology names below. Product identity is a taxonomy fact, not an evidence claim: a product being part of the catalogue does not mean any particular workload is eligible, validated, value-verified, deployed or licensable.

## Canonical technologies

The current technology taxonomy is:

- **PRISM** — observation technology / architecture behind ASTOP.
- **AXIOM**
- **AXIOM-TENSOR**
- **CRE**
- **FQNM**
- **QNTA**

PRISM, AXIOM, AXIOM-TENSOR, CRE, FQNM and QNTA are technologies. **They are not separately sold products.** Their applicability and evidence are workload- and source-specific; this taxonomy must not be used to imply that they all apply together.

## Two intervention domains

### Observation before reasoning

PRISM structures decision-relevant observation before expensive reasoning. ASTOP is the product that operationalizes that observation domain. Public visitors may know ASTOP exists and may receive public-safe value/evidence explanations.

### Representation before execution

ALPHA Compute addresses eligible computational workloads before execution through appropriate technologies such as AXIOM, AXIOM-TENSOR, CRE, FQNM and QNTA where each technology's own eligibility conditions are satisfied. ALPHA Core is separate and optional.

## Independence and commercial sequence

ASTOP, ALPHA Compute and ALPHA Core are technically independent products. Current commercialization may use ASTOP as the first controlled proof point, followed by a **separate** ALPHA Compute qualification when a deeper computational workload remains, and ALPHA Core only when validated ALPHA Compute evidence supports a hardware case.

Therefore:

- discussion is not opportunity creation;
- interest is not qualification;
- account creation is not a product route;
- ASTOP success does not automatically establish ALPHA Compute eligibility;
- ALPHA Compute does not automatically imply ALPHA Core.

## Public-safe commercial boundary

ASTOP is not presented as a public self-service checkout/download product. Executables, enabling materials, production rights, protected implementation information, account-specific pricing and negotiated rights remain controlled by the applicable authorization, evaluation and contract state. An NDA can protect an authorized disclosure but does not itself create access to restricted material.

## Evidence discipline

No technology is universally applicable. Mathematical or structural eligibility precedes performance claims. Quantitative advantage must remain tied to the demonstrated workload, baseline, correctness/fidelity conditions and evidence status. A product name must never be used as shorthand for "validated", "value-verified" or "licensable".
