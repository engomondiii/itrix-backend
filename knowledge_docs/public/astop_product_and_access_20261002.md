# ASTOP observation and access
Sources: ASTOP White Paper v2.3; Customer Journey v1.5; Website License Order Agreement v2.6; Software Protection Policy v1.5. The License Order governs pricing and seat scope.

## How it works
ASTOP is an Observation Layer for AI agents and long-running computational systems. Acquire evidence from logs, APIs, files, counters and process state; detect decision-relevant semantic change; deliver sufficient observations to reasoning. Low-level sensing continues outside expensive reasoning. ASTOP does not replace the runtime, scheduler, tool contract or reasoning policy.
Strong fits have long-running work, redundant state and sparse required events. Short jobs, event-dense work, ambiguous telemetry and costly observation logic can be weak fits. Required events and correct decisions must be preserved before any efficiency claim.

## Current benchmark evidence
White Paper v2.3 and the supplied R07 PRISM manuscript report finite-panel reductions of 58.8–69.2% in observation-induced tokens, 23.1–30.1% in model calls and 88.1–90.0% in delivered bytes. The local Qwen3.5 panel uses 9B, 27B and 35B models; all 360 reported episodes succeeded. These are descriptive benchmark observations, not population claims or guarantees for a customer's workload.
ML training used more calls under PRISM despite fewer tokens. Controlled durations of 30, 60, 120 and 300 seconds illustrate finite trends, not proof of an asymptotic limit or 24-hour endurance. An additional 18-episode LLM-free panel covers SGD, Rust compilation and numerical simulation. Observer CPU, memory, I/O, classification cost and latency count against net value.
The older 51.9–84.5% band belongs to earlier evidence and must not be presented as the latest benchmark. Do not add benchmark percentages to SoL-Pi, caching or other tools' reported savings.

## Access and current standard terms
Public questions do not require an account. When a visitor chooses ordinary access, select Individual or Organization. Protected enterprise evaluation is a separately approved exception for a concrete requirement the ordinary path cannot address, not a third retail license. Standard LO v2.6 pricing is USD 20 for one individual user and USD 16 per authorized user for an organization purchasing two or more seats. An eligible Branch referral gives a 10% customer discount; discounts do not stack by default and the greater applicable discount governs. A qualifying organization can still carry referral attribution.
Prices and totals must be shown before acceptance. Enterprise scope is separately agreed.
Legal counterparty verification and verified email precede exact LO acceptance; verified payment precedes license creation; an active entitlement precedes protected download and activation. Purchasing a file does not transfer ownership or redistribution rights. Organization users are identified, seats are not pooled, and reassignment terminates the prior user's access.
The LO allows refund requests received within 30 calendar days, subject to its terms and mandatory rights. Approval immediately terminates the affected entitlement, including organization seats unless an explicit partial refund is approved. Renewal stops on approval while previously issued offline validity remains bounded by fourteen days from the last successful validation. A job started while entitled may finish; no new session may start after expiry without restored entitlement. Legal rights end on approval; do not promise instant offline erasure or terminate existing jobs solely for revocation.
Checkout, downloads and activation are available only when the deployment's required services and approved legal text are configured. Documents do not prove a live payment service exists.

## Customer proof
Connect one workload, define events that must not be suppressed, compare a fair baseline, verify task quality, then subtract observer overhead and latency. Continue, tune, try another workload, stop/refund under applicable terms or expand on measured value. A no-fit outcome is valid.
